The Story

Built by a mom
who needed it

"I'm a stay-at-home mom of two boys, one autistic. I built Known in my free time because I needed it. I had facts, I had tabs, I had voice memos, I had screenshots — but I couldn't connect them. This is the app I wish existed."
— Jess, Creator of Known

Known is built for the people who can't let go of a question until they have the full picture. Whether that's a parent researching treatments for their kid, a citizen journalist following a thread that won't quit, or a student who goes five layers deeper than the assignment requires — this app is yours.
