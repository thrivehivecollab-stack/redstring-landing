Security Infrastructure

LOCK IT
DOWN.

Investigating powerful people requires protecting your work from them. Red String is built with that assumption from the ground up.

```
BIOMETRIC LOCK
Face ID and Touch ID on every launch. Your investigation never visible without your face or finger.
DECOY MODE
Wrong PIN shows a completely different innocent-looking app. Your real investigations are invisible.
INVISIBLE INK
Mark any node as invisible. It shows as a blank card. Content only appears while you actively hold it — release and it vanishes.
SCREENSHOT BLOCKING
Screenshots are blocked system-wide within the app. What's on your canvas stays on your canvas.
AES-256 ENCRYPTION
All investigation data encrypted at rest. Tips end-to-end encrypted — only your device can decrypt them.
FORENSIC WATERMARKING
Every export invisibly watermarked with recipient identity. If your work leaks, Red String tells you exactly who did it.
PERMISSION LAYERS
Granular control per user and per role. Who can view, save, export, screenshot, or share — all configurable per investigation.
OWNER NOTIFICATIONS
Real-time push alert every time someone views, downloads, shares, or screenshots anything in your investigation.
```
