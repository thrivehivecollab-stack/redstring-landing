The Origin

Born From The
Search For Truth.

■ FROM THE CREATOR

"I'm a stay-at-home mom. A self-taught researcher. Someone who loves a deep dive and can't let a loose thread go. For years I followed independent investigative journalists and citizen researchers doing extraordinary work — connecting dots across documents, social media, and public records that mainstream outlets wouldn't touch.

"The problem was always the same: the evidence lived everywhere and the workspace lived nowhere. Notes in one app, links in another, screenshots in a camera roll, theories in a group chat. I wanted one place where all of it could live together — organized, connected, searchable, and secure. So I built it."

— Jess, Creator of Red String Research

From Research to Real Change

Your Work Deserves
To Be Heard.

Red String is not just a place to organize what you find. It is a launchpad for what you do with it.

```
■ THE PROBLEM

Citizen researchers do extraordinary work. They connect dots that mainstream media won't touch, source evidence that institutions ignore, and build timelines that tell the full story. And then it sits in a group chat. Or disappears into a thread. Or never reaches the people with the platform and power to amplify it.

■ THE RED STRING DIFFERENCE

"What if your entire investigation — every node, every connection, every source, every timeline — could be packaged into a professional intelligence report and delivered to the journalists, podcasters, lawyers, and officials who have the reach and authority to take it further?"

📋
Intelligence Dossier Export
Generate a classified-style PDF report from your entire investigation. Cover sheet, executive summary, entity profiles, evidence section, source chain, timeline, and chain of custody. The kind of document that gets taken seriously in a newsroom, a courtroom, or a congressional office.
🤖
AI-Assisted Outreach
Tell the AI what you have found and what outcome you want. It identifies who to contact — which journalists cover this beat, which podcasters have the right audience, which lawyers handle this type of case, which officials have jurisdiction — and drafts the outreach for you. Professional, targeted, and ready to send.
📡
Broadcast Your Findings
Go live from your investigation canvas. Walk your audience through the evidence in real time. Let viewers see exactly how the dots connect as you explain it. Turn your research session into content that reaches thousands.
🤝
Collaborate With Other Investigators
Invite others into your War Room. Work the same canvas together in real time. Combine what you each know into one unified investigation. The collective intelligence of a community, organized in one place.
Citizen Research. Professional Impact.
The truth has always been out there. The people finding it have always been ordinary citizens. Red String gives that work the structure, the presentation, and the reach it deserves — so it can no longer be ignored.
```
